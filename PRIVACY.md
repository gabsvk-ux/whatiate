# whatIAte privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

whatIAte is a personal food diary for Android, made for its author's own use.

## What the app stores

The diary: the names of foods, optional descriptions, optional photos, and the day and meal each belongs to. All of it is stored on the phone, in the app's private storage.

## Google Drive

Backup to Google Drive is optional and off until you connect it.

When connected, the app asks for one permission, `drive.file`. With it the app can create and update only the files and folders it made itself. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Google Drive.

The app uses this permission for one purpose: to copy your diary (photos and a data file) into a folder in your own Google Drive.

## What is shared

Nothing. The diary is not sent to the developer or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The only place data leaves the phone for is your own Google Drive, and only if you connect it.

## Removing your data

- Deleting an entry in the app removes it from the phone.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.
- The backup folder in Google Drive is yours; delete it in Google Drive whenever you like.
- You can withdraw the app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
